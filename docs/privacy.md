# Privacy Policy — Classy-O 80

Last updated: 27 September 2026

**Classy-O 80 collects no data.**

The app is a calculator and nothing else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no third-party SDKs and no networking code of any kind. Nothing you type is transmitted
anywhere, because the app cannot transmit anything.

## What is stored on your device

So that the calculator comes back the way you left it, the app saves three things in its own
private storage on your device:

- the value in independent memory (`M+` / `M−` / `MRC`),
- any exchange rates you stored in `C1`, `C2` or `C3`,
- whether the calculator is in normal or conversion mode.

This is stored locally, is readable only by this app, is included in your device's own backups if
you use iCloud Backup or iTunes/Finder backups, and is deleted when you delete the app. It is never
sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. As it collects no data, it collects none from
children either.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects or transmits anything, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
